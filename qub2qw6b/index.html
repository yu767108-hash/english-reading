US Inflation Comes In Higher Than Expected in October

BBC News · 约 3 分钟
导读　美国 10 月消费者价格指数环比上涨 0.4%，高于分析师预期的 0.3%，中期选举刚过一周便给美联储 12 月加息增添了理由。文章解释了通胀如何用「一篮子商品」衡量、需求与供给如何推高物价，以及关税为何让美国企业承担了近九成成本。

Prices in the United States rose faster than expected last month, official figures show. The consumer price index climbed 0.4% in October, down from 0.6% in September but ahead of the 0.3% that analysts had predicted. The surprise reading lands a week after midterm elections dominated by the cost of living, and it may strengthen the case for the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates when it meets in December.

美国官方数据显示，上月物价上涨速度快于预期。10 月消费者价格指数上涨 0.4%，低于 9 月的 0.6%，但高于分析师预测的 0.3%。这个出人意料的读数出现在中期选举一周之后——那场选举的焦点正是生活成本——它可能增强美联储在 12 月会议上加息的论据。

Inflation measures how quickly prices rise over time. If a bottle of milk costs $1 today and $1.10 a year from now, annual inflation for that bottle is 10%. The Bureau of Labor Statistics tracks this by following a basket of everyday items - fresh food, petrol, housing and medical care - and updating the basket as spending habits change.

通货膨胀衡量的是物价随时间的上涨速度。如果一瓶牛奶今天卖 1 美元，一年后涨到 1.10 美元，那么这瓶牛奶的年通胀率就是 10%。美国劳工统计局通过跟踪一篮子日常商品——新鲜食品、汽油、住房和医疗服务——并随着消费习惯的变化不断更新这个篮子，来衡量通胀。

Several forces push prices up. When demand outstrips supply, producers can charge more; the craze for Labubu dolls and their sky-high resale prices is one example. Higher incomes can fuel demand in the same way. Supply shocks matter too: crude oil traded just above $70 a barrel before the US-Israel war with Iran, and as the conflict constricted supplies the price jumped past $100.

推高物价的因素有好几个。当需求超过供给时，生产商就可以提高价格；近年来拉布布娃娃的热潮及其高得离谱的转售价格就是一个例子。收入提高同样会刺激需求，进而推高价格。供给冲击也很重要：在与伊朗开战之前，原油价格略高于每桶 70 美元，而随着冲突收紧了供应，油价迅速突破 100 美元。

October's increase was driven by housing, food and energy. Energy costs, however, rose just 0.7%, a far cry from September's 5.2% jump. Because the bureau did not collect data in October 2025, it could not publish a 12-month figure for last month; the rate for the year to September stood at 3.7%.

10 月的涨幅主要由住房、食品和能源成本推动。不过能源价格仅上涨 0.7%，远低于 9 月 5.2% 的涨幅。由于劳工统计局没有收集 2025 年 10 月的数据，因此无法给出上月的 12 个月同比数据；截至 9 月的年度通胀率为 3.7%。

Tariffs are part of the story. President Trump has placed levies on most trading partners, arguing that they would raise tax revenue and revive manufacturing. Research by the New York Fed suggests exporters barely cut their prices in response, so American firms absorbed almost 90% of the added cost. Every one percentage point rise in average tariffs, the study found, pushed consumer prices up by nearly 0.25% within a year.

关税也是其中一环。特朗普总统对大多数贸易伙伴加征了关税，称这将增加税收、重振制造业。纽约联储的研究显示，出口商几乎没有因此降价，因此美国企业承担了近 90% 的额外成本。研究发现，平均关税每提高 1 个百分点，一年内就会把消费品价格推高近 0.25%。

Inflation is still above the Fed's 2% target, though far below the roughly 9% peak reached in 2022, when pandemic supply chains buckled and oil surged after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Slower inflation does not mean falling prices; it means prices are climbing more gently. Central banks generally regard about 2% as healthy - high enough to keep shoppers spending, low enough to stay out of their decisions.

通胀仍高于美联储 2% 的目标，但远低于 2022 年约 9% 的峰值——当时疫情扰乱了供应链，俄罗斯全面入侵乌克兰又令油价飙升。通胀放缓并不意味着物价下跌，而是意味着物价上涨得更温和。各国央行通常认为 2% 左右是健康的：足以让人们继续消费，又低到不会左右他们的决定。

M John Harrison Becomes First Science Fiction Author to Win the Booker Prize

The Guardian · 约 3 分钟
导读　81 岁的英国作家 M·约翰·哈里森凭末日小说《万物的终结》赢得 2026 年布克奖，成为该奖历史上最年长的得主，也是首位真正出自科幻圈的获奖者。评委会称赞这本书让人天翻地覆却又留有一丝乐观，并指出在 AI 时代它显得格外应景。

M John Harrison has won this year's Booker Prize for The End of Everything, a post-apocalyptic novel set on the Kent coast a few decades from now. At 81, he becomes the oldest winner in the prize's history, overtaking Margaret Atwood, who was 79 when she shared the award in 2019. Harrison collected the £50,000 prize at a ceremony in London on Monday evening.

M·约翰·哈里森凭《万物的终结》赢得今年的布克奖。这部末日之后的小说，背景设在几十年后的肯特海岸。81 岁的他成为该奖历史上年龄最大的得主，超过了玛格丽特·阿特伍德——她在 2019 年分享该奖时 79 岁。周一晚，哈里森在伦敦的颁奖典礼上领取了 5 万英镑奖金。

Gaby Wood, chief executive of the Booker Prize Foundation, said many past winners had dabbled in science fiction, but Harrison was 'the first Booker winner to have emerged from the bona fide SF world'. She called it 'a historic win, for a writer whose decades of work are lightly worn on every page'.

布克奖基金会首席执行官加比·伍德说，过去许多获奖者都涉猎过科幻小说，但哈里森是「第一位真正出自正统战科幻世界的布克奖得主」。她称这是「一场历史性的胜利，属于一位数十年创作功力在每一页上都举重若轻的作家」。

The novel follows Phillip Tennent and his ageing aunt Marnie as they try to survive years after a disaster nobody can properly explain. Around them loom the iGhetti, vast, shape-changing beings whose origins and intentions are never revealed. At 198 pages it was the shortest book on the shortlist, and Harrison's first nomination.

小说讲述菲利普·坦南特和他年迈的姑妈玛妮，在一场无人能真正解释清楚的灾难过去多年后努力求生。他们周围笼罩着 iGhetti——那些庞大、会变形的生物，其来历和意图始终没有揭开。全书 198 页，是入围名单中最短的一部，也是哈里森首次获得提名。

Mary Beard, who chaired the judges, said the decision was unanimous after a full day of discussion. 'It's a book that turns you upside down, raises a wry smile and, surprisingly given the subject matter, leaves you with a twinge of optimism,' she said. 'One main point is that if, or when, such an apocalypse comes, it will be incomprehensible. The old rules won't matter either.'

评委会主席玛丽·比尔德说，经过一整天的讨论，评委们一致作出了决定。「这本书会让你天翻地覆，让你苦笑一下，而且出乎意料的是，考虑到它的题材，还会给你留下一丝乐观，」她说。「书中的一个要点是：如果、或者说当这样的末日来临时，它将是无法理解的。旧的规则也不再适用。」

Born in Warwickshire in 1945, Harrison sold his first story in 1965 and spent much of the 1970s running the books pages of New Worlds, a speculative fiction magazine. Since then he has published five story collections and an anti-memoir, Wish I Was Here. His novels have won a string of awards, from the Boardman Tasker prize to the Goldsmiths prize.

哈里森 1945 年生于沃里克郡，1965 年卖出第一个短篇，1970 年代的大部分时间在主编推想小说杂志《新世界》的书评版面。此后他出版了五部短篇集和一部「反回忆录」《但愿我在这里》。他的小说赢得了一连串奖项，从博德曼-塔斯克奖到戈德史密斯奖。

The win is a rare moment of mainstream recognition for a writer long revered by his peers but kept at the margins of literary fiction. 'Why shouldn't a well written work of SF be considered literary fiction?' he asked on BBC Radio 4 last month. Even admirers admit the book is demanding: the critic Michel Faber warned that it 'won't be for everyone'.

这次获奖，对一位长期受同行推崇、却始终处在文学小说边缘地带的作家来说，是难得的主流认可。「一部写得好的科幻作品，为什么不能被视为文学小说呢？」他上个月在 BBC 广播四台问道。就连欣赏它的人也承认这本书并不轻松：评论家米歇尔·法伯提醒说，它「不适合所有人」。

The prize also feels timely in the age of AI. Beard noted that the 'i' in iGhetti hints at something digital without ever confirming it. The judges weighed 163 books published in the UK or Ireland over the past year. It is the first Booker win for Serpent's Tail, an imprint of the independent publisher Profile Books.

在人工智能时代，这个奖也显得恰逢其时。比尔德指出，iGhetti 中的「i」暗示着某种数字化的东西，却又从不点明。评委们从过去一年在英国或爱尔兰出版的 163 部作品中作出选择。这也是独立出版社 Profile Books 旗下品牌 Serpent's Tail 首次赢得布克奖。
