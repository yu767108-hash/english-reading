Anthropic Heads for Wall Street as Voters Turn Against AI

CNBC · 约 3 分钟
导读　美国中期选举刚结束，选民对科技富豪和 AI 数据中心的抵触情绪明显上升。偏偏在这个节点，Anthropic 准备登陆纳斯达克，估值目标高达 1 万亿至 1.5 万亿美元。文章梳理了市场的紧张情绪、数据中心引发的民怨，以及一家 AI 公司走向公开市场后要面对的更严苛审视。

Americans went to the polls this week and sent a blunt message: they are tired of tech billionaires and uneasy about artificial intelligence. Against that background, Anthropic, one of the world's best-known AI developers, is preparing to go public.

本周，美国选民参加了投票，发出了一个直白的信号：他们厌倦了科技亿万富翁，对人工智能也感到不安。在这样的背景下，全球最知名的 AI 公司之一 Anthropic 正准备上市。

The company is aiming for a Nasdaq listing in mid-November. Investors reportedly expect a valuation of $1 trillion to $1.5 trillion, down from the $2 trillion floated in recent months. It would be the first pure-play AI company to reach the public market.

该公司计划于 11 月中旬在纳斯达克上市。据报道，投资者预期其估值在 1 万亿至 1.5 万亿美元之间，低于近几个月传出的 2 万亿美元。它将成为首家登陆公开市场的纯 AI 公司。

The timing is awkward. The Nasdaq has just suffered its steepest one-day fall in 19 months, and October was its worst month since March 2025. Chipmakers and neoclouds, the firms most closely tied to the AI buildout, have been hit hardest.

时机颇为尴尬。纳斯达克刚刚经历了 19 个月来最惨的单日下跌，10 月也是自 2025 年 3 月以来表现最差的一个月。与 AI 基建关系最紧密的芯片制造商和「新云」厂商受到的冲击最大。

Voters in Tuesday's midterm elections gave Democrats sweeping victories in Congress, and candidates who wanted stricter AI rules won comfortably in Michigan, Ohio, Texas and Georgia. Communities rejected data-center measures in 40 of 41 local ballots. More than half of voters called AI data centers a major factor in their choice.

在周二的期中选举中，民主党在国会取得压倒性胜利，主张对 AI 加强监管的候选人在密歇根、俄亥俄、得克萨斯和佐治亚轻松获胜。41 场地方公投中有 40 场否决了数据中心相关议案。超过一半的选民表示，AI 数据中心是他们做出选择的主要因素之一。

"People don't want a data center near them if it is only going to house AI that helps a big tech company make a profit," said Maura Austin, a behavioural scientist at the University of Virginia. Listing at such a high valuation, she added, pushes Anthropic towards the caricature of big tech and billionaires.

「如果数据中心只是用来容纳帮助大型科技公司赚钱的 AI，人们就不希望它建在自己附近，」弗吉尼亚大学行为科学家莫拉·奥斯汀说。她还表示，以如此高的估值上市，会把 Anthropic 推向「大型科技公司加亿万富翁」的漫画式形象。

Anthropic has long presented itself as a safety-conscious company. Its chief executive, Dario Amodei, co-founded it with researchers who left OpenAI, now its main rival. The firm is best known for its Claude models and sells mainly to other businesses, which has kept it out of the public eye. A listing will change that, bringing far more scrutiny. Analysts note that public markets also demand transparency, and a debut can win new users.

Anthropic 长期以来把自己塑造成一家注重安全的公司。其首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代伊与几位离开 OpenAI 的研究员共同创立了该公司，OpenAI 如今是它的主要竞争对手。Anthropic 以 Claude 系列模型闻名，主要向其他企业销售，这使它一直远离公众视野。上市将改变这一点，带来更多的审视。分析人士指出，公开市场也要求透明度，而一次成功的上市还能赢得新用户。

The backlash grew over the summer after several high-profile safety incidents. Concern about data centers has intensified as the biggest cloud providers lift spending into the hundreds of billions of dollars; Anthropic alone says it has committed about $895 billion to computing and infrastructure. Many Americans fear new facilities will raise utility bills and cost jobs.

今年夏天，几起引人注目的安全事故之后，反对声浪不断高涨。随着最大的云服务商把支出提高到数千亿美元级别，人们对数据中心的担忧也在加剧；仅 Anthropic 一家就表示已在计算和基础设施上投入约 8950 亿美元。许多美国人担心新建设施会推高电费、减少就业。

In its prospectus, Anthropic admits that opposition to data centers has become better organised. A survey published on Monday by the University of Southern California found that 71% of respondents did not trust big tech to act in society's interest on data-center development. "Once you've lost trust, it's hard to gain it back," said professor Wandi Bruine de Bruin.

在招股说明书中，Anthropic 承认反对数据中心的情绪已变得更有组织。南加州大学周一发布的一项调查发现，71% 的受访者不信任大型科技公司会以符合社会利益的方式推进数据中心建设。「一旦失去信任，就很难再赢回来，」温迪·布鲁因·德布鲁因教授说。

The Bantu Expansion: How One Migration Remade a Continent

Ars Technica · 约 4 分钟
导读　铁矿石极难冶炼，但大津巴布韦的工匠却造出了能自行抽风的巨型熔炉。这样的技术随一支来自西非的人群——班图语使用者——扩散到非洲南半部。语言学、考古学和遗传学三条线索，正在一点点拼出这场持续数千年的大迁徙。

Extracting iron from its ore is not easy, but the people of Great Zimbabwe, capital of a kingdom that thrived in southern Africa from 1000 to 1700 AD, were masters at it. Near the city's stone ruins, clusters of smelting furnaces survive as rubble, some almost an adult's arm span wide, their thick walls built to hold heat above 2,100 degrees Fahrenheit.

从矿石中提炼铁并不容易，但大津巴布韦的人们却是此中高手——那里曾是一个公元 1000 年至 1700 年间繁荣于非洲南部的王国的都城。在城市的石头废墟附近，成群的冶炼炉以瓦砾的形态留存下来，有些几乎有一个成年人臂展那么宽，厚厚的炉壁能把温度维持在 2100 华氏度以上。

Unlike furnaces that needed workers to pump air in with bellows, these drew air in and pushed it out through a chimney on their own. That natural draft made iron production far easier, and the metal became hoes, axes and arrowheads. "These furnaces indicate intensive and technologically sophisticated iron production," says archaeometallurgist Ezekiel Mtetwa.

与需要工人用风箱鼓风的炉子不同，这些炉子能自行吸入空气，并通过烟囱排出。这种自然抽风让炼铁变得容易得多，铁被制成锄头、斧头和箭头。「这些炉子说明当时存在着密集且技术成熟的铁生产，」考古冶金学家埃泽基尔·姆泰特瓦说。

Researchers trace this and other iron-working societies to an adaptable, innovative group from western Africa: Bantu speakers. Starting 6,000 to 4,000 years ago, they spread gradually across the southern half of the continent, bringing a sedentary way of life based on farming, livestock, pottery and metalworking. Today around 350 million Africans speak one of roughly 500 Bantu languages, and at least as many people carry Bantu ancestry in their DNA.

研究人员把这种以及其他冶铁社会追溯到来自西非的一个适应力强、富有创新精神的群体：班图语使用者。从 6000 到 4000 年前开始，他们逐渐扩散到非洲大陆的南半部，带来了以农耕、畜牧、制陶和金属加工为基础的定居生活方式。今天，约有 3.5 亿非洲人使用大约 500 种班图语言中的一种，携带班图祖先 DNA 的人至少也有这么多。

Reconstructing the expansion is a hard puzzle: written records are sparse and funding is limited. Linguists, archaeologists and geneticists have joined forces to unearth clues. The first evidence came from the striking similarity of Bantu languages: in 1497, a Portuguese crew member who had lived in west-central Africa could reportedly talk with locals in the south-east.

还原这场扩张是一个难题：文字记录稀少，资金也有限。语言学家、考古学家和遗传学家联手寻找线索。最早的证据来自班图语言之间惊人的相似性：据报道，1497 年，一名曾在中西部非洲生活过的葡萄牙船员能够与东南部的当地人交谈。

Archaeologists then found pottery of related but varied styles, along with the earliest traces of village life, metal-making, domesticated animals and western African crops, all within the last 2,000 years. Northern sites were generally older, showing a clear north-to-south progression. In the 1990s, some scholars doubted that people had moved at all, arguing that skills and languages could have spread between settled communities.

随后，考古学家发现了风格相关但又有差异的陶器，以及村庄生活、金属制造、家畜饲养和西非作物最早留下的痕迹，这些都在最近 2000 年之内。北部的遗址普遍更古老，呈现出清晰的由北向南推进的过程。20 世纪 90 年代，一些学者怀疑人口是否真的发生过迁移，他们认为技能和语言也可能在定居社群之间传播。

Genetics settled the argument. A 2009 study of 2,432 Africans found that Bantu speakers from west, central and southern Africa are far more closely related than their distance apart would suggest. A 2024 analysis of 1,763 genomes found the greatest diversity in western Africa, declining with distance: the classic signature of a population expanding from one homeland, most likely the Grassfields region of Cameroon.

遗传学平息了这场争论。2009 年一项针对 2432 名非洲人的研究发现，来自西部、中部和南部非洲的班图语使用者之间的亲缘关系，远比他们之间的地理距离所显示的更为接近。2024 年对 1763 个基因组的一项分析发现，多样性在西部非洲最高，并随距离递减：这是一个种群从单一故乡向外扩张的典型特征，那个故乡最可能是喀麦隆的格拉斯菲尔德地区。

Iron smelters near Great Zimbabwe apparently added bone fragments to their furnaces, perhaps as a blessing: bone contains calcium, which helps separate iron from ore. Much remains uncertain, and money is still short, but the picture is slowly coming into focus.

大津巴布韦附近的炼铁者显然会在炉中加入骨碎片，也许是为了祈福：骨骼中含有钙，有助于把铁从矿石中分离出来。许多问题仍不确定，资金也依然紧张，但图景正在慢慢清晰起来。
