Weakening AI copyright law will spell ‘disaster for creativity’, global music body warns

The Guardian · 约 3 分钟
导读　澳大利亚正在讨论放宽版权规则，以便 AI 公司用本地素材训练模型。代表全球唱片业的 IFPI 公开警告：这会成为“最薄弱的一环”，并引发其他国家效仿。文章还呈现了 AI 公司、创作者与政府三方的立场。

Australia is considering weaker copyright rules to help artificial intelligence companies, and the global music industry is watching closely. The International Federation of the Phonographic Industry (IFPI), which speaks for record labels around the world, says the plan would be ridiculous and dangerous, and a disaster for creativity.

澳大利亚正在考虑放宽版权规则以帮助人工智能公司，全球音乐产业正在密切关注。代表世界各地唱片公司的国际唱片业协会（IFPI）表示，这一计划“荒谬且危险”，对创造力是一场灾难。

“Why would a sophisticated, democratic country with a phenomenal cultural power of its own make this very odd decision?” asked Victoria Oakley, the IFPI's chief executive. “I don't think Australia wants to be the weakest link — the one that believed the promises and threw creativity under the bus on behalf of everyone else.”

“一个成熟、民主、本身就拥有强大文化影响力的国家，为什么要做出这么奇怪的决定？”IFPI 首席执行官维多利亚·奥克利问道。“我不认为澳大利亚想成为最薄弱的一环——那个相信了承诺、代表所有人把创造力抛弃掉的国家。”

The debate is part of a wider set of AI policies being prepared by the Labor government, covering data centres, online safety, jobs and energy. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has promised the strongest possible protection and said it would be theft if creators were not paid. Yet creative industries were alarmed when AI companies asked for weaker laws so they could train their models on Australian material.

这场争论是工党政府正在准备的一系列 AI 政策的一部分，涉及数据中心、网络安全、就业和能源。总理安东尼·阿尔巴尼斯承诺提供“最强保护”，并表示如果创作者得不到报酬，那就等同于“盗窃”。然而，当 AI 公司要求放宽法律、以便用澳大利亚的素材训练模型时，创意产业感到震惊。

Some AI firms refuse to sign licensing deals under existing rules, arguing that they are too difficult. Anthropic has suggested an opt-out model, which would let it scrape content unless rights holders object. Large AI companies say they need legal certainty before they invest in Australia or train their models there.

一些 AI 公司拒绝按现行规则签署授权协议，称这太难操作。Anthropic 提出了一种“退出”模式：除非权利人明确反对，否则它可以抓取内容。大型 AI 公司表示，在澳大利亚投资或训练模型之前，需要法律上的确定性。

Oakley said similar requests had been rejected in the United States, Britain and other countries. “I just don't think it's true that you will only get investment or growth if you entirely give in to the demands of AI companies,” she said. She also fears contagion: if Australia gives way, other governments may follow, and copyrighted work everywhere could suffer. She said the IFPI had been asking governments around the world: “Are you seeing what is going on in Australia?”

奥克利说，类似的要求在美国、英国和其他国家都曾被拒绝。“我完全不认为，只有完全屈服于 AI 公司的要求，才能获得投资或增长，”她说。她还担心“传染效应”：如果澳大利亚让步，其他政府可能跟进，全球的版权作品都可能受损。她说，IFPI 一直在问各国政府：“你们看到澳大利亚正在发生什么了吗？”

In answers to a parliamentary committee, OpenAI argued that Spotify's business is different, because it mainly transmits unchanged music and should therefore license what it streams. Oakley rejected that logic, noting that music labels have already signed dozens of deals with smaller AI companies around the world. OpenAI was responding to questions from independent senator David Pocock.

在回答议会委员会质询时，OpenAI 辩称 Spotify 的商业模式不同：它主要传输未经改动的音乐，因此应当为其播放的内容获得授权。奥克利驳斥了这一逻辑，并指出唱片公司已经在全球与多家小型 AI 公司签署了数十份协议。OpenAI 是在回答独立参议员大卫·波科克的提问时作出上述表示的。

The attorney general's office says the government is committed to supporting creators, and that any future changes will deliver meaningful control and fair compensation. Oakley remains worried. “It would be a huge mistake,” she said, “and I think the prize is not worth what you're throwing away.”

司法部长办公室表示，政府致力于支持创作者，未来的任何修改都将带来“有意义的控制权和公平的报酬”。奥克利仍然担忧。“那将是一个巨大的错误，”她说，“而且我认为，得到的东西不值得你为此放弃的一切。”

Bluey’s Daley Pearson on how losing his brother inspired his new film

The Guardian · 约 3 分钟
导读　《布鲁伊》主创戴利·皮尔森推出首部真人电影《Allen》：一个男人童年遇见外星人，成年后仍不肯放手。影片充满 80 年代怀旧元素，背后却是他 13 岁时哥哥因癌症去世的真实伤痛。

Daley Pearson helped create Bluey, one of the most popular children's shows in the world. Now the co-founder of Brisbane's Ludo Studio has made his first live-action film, Allen, for Netflix — a family story about growing up and refusing to let go. Pearson co-directed the film with Jeffrey Walker and wrote it with Sophie Miller.

戴利·皮尔森参与创作了《布鲁伊》——全球最受欢迎的儿童节目之一。如今，这位布里斯班 Ludo 工作室的联合创始人推出了他的第一部真人电影《Allen》，由 Netflix 发行，讲述一个关于成长与不愿放手的故事。皮尔森与杰弗里·沃克共同执导，并与索菲·米勒共同编剧。

As a boy in the small coastal town of Coolum, Petey meets a friendly, bug-eyed alien. The creature cannot quite say “alien”, so they call him Allen. For one happy month they share snacks, videos and board games — until Allen is suddenly called home. Decades later, Petey is stuck: obsessed, unable to move on, and frustrating everyone around him. Then Allen returns. The opening scene moves through his old tapes, games and action figures.

在沿海小镇库勒姆长大的男孩皮蒂，遇到了一只友善的凸眼外星人。小家伙发不出“alien”这个音，于是他们叫他 Allen。在一个快乐的月里，他们一起分享零食、录像带和桌游——直到 Allen 突然被召回。几十年后，皮蒂困在原地：执念、无法前行，也让身边的人疲惫不堪。然后，Allen 回来了。影片开场镜头扫过他收藏的旧录像带、游戏和玩偶。

The film borrows heavily from ET, from its story to its music and even its poster, where two fingers reach towards each other. Pearson calls the references “pretty aggressive”. He says the film is built from the things he loved as a child — ET, board games, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, The Lion King — and that 1980s pop culture was “the best thing you could ever want”.

这部电影大量借鉴《ET》：从情节到配乐，甚至海报上两根手指相触的画面。皮尔森称这些致敬“相当直接”。他说，电影由他童年热爱的东西构成——《ET》、桌游、《忍者神龟》、《狮子王》——并称 1980 年代的流行文化是“你能想到的最棒的东西”。

But the story has a deeper source. When Pearson was 13, his older brother Liam died of cancer at 26. For years he rarely spoke about it. Making Allen helped him understand how the loss had shaped him. “He was my best friend, the best thing ever to me,” he says. The film, he explains, is about “the perfume of Liam” — the feeling of losing something and wanting it back so badly that you stop living.

但这个故事有更深的来源。皮尔森 13 岁时，哥哥利亚姆因癌症去世，年仅 26 岁。多年来他很少谈起这件事。拍摄《Allen》帮助他理解这场失去如何塑造了自己。“他是我最好的朋友，是我拥有过最好的东西，”他说。他解释，这部电影讲的是“利亚姆的气息”——那种失去某样东西、极其渴望它回来，以至于停止生活的感觉。

Pearson admits that he listens only to old songs and keeps old toys around the office. “It connects me to a past that doesn't exist any more. And it's bad,” he says. He also produced the 2022 horror hit Talk To Me, another story about being unable to let go — a connection he had never noticed until it was pointed out.

皮尔森承认，他只听老歌，办公室里也摆满旧玩具。“它把我连接到一个已经不存在的过去。而这并不好，”他说。他还担任了 2022 年恐怖片《Talk To Me》的制片人——那同样是一个关于无法放手的故事，而这个联系他此前从未意识到。

Allen is still a comedy for children, with “a silly, stupid alien pulling the carpet out from people constantly”. Pearson hopes kids enjoy the jokes, while their parents notice the quieter sadness underneath. “I hope you press play and have a giddy time,” he says. Allen is available on Netflix now.

《Allen》仍然是一部给孩子看的喜剧，有“一个傻乎乎的外星人不断把人们脚下的地毯抽走”。皮尔森希望孩子们喜欢这些笑点，而父母能注意到下面更安静的悲伤。“我希望你按下播放键，度过一段晕乎乎的快乐时光，”他说。《Allen》目前已在 Netflix 上线。
