Sony's $99 Pulse Headset Skips the Extras and Nails the Basics

索尼推出售价 99.99 美元的 PlayStation Pulse 游戏耳机，放弃降噪、可换电池等花哨功能，只专注音质与舒适。它用上了此前只在高端产品上出现的平面磁驱动单元，并可通过 PlayStation Link 与蓝牙同时播放两路音频。文章认为，对预算有限的 PS5 玩家来说，这是一个稳妥的选择。

01The pitch

Gaming headsets often promise to do everything at once: noise cancellation, studio-quality sound, swappable batteries and connections to every console you own. Sony's new PlayStation Pulse takes a narrower approach. At $99.99, it offers two things done well, audio and comfort, and very little else. That restraint may be its strongest selling point, especially for players who want a solid headset without paying flagship prices.

游戏耳机常常承诺样样全能：主动降噪、录音室级音质、可更换电池，还能连上你拥有的每一台游戏机。索尼新推出的 PlayStation Pulse 走的是更窄的路线：99.99 美元的价格，只把音质和佩戴舒适这两件事做好，其他几乎一概不加。这种克制或许正是它最大的卖点，尤其对那些想要一副靠谱耳机、又不想付旗舰价钱的玩家来说。
00:30

02The sound

Inside its plain ear cups sit custom planar magnetic drivers, which produce clean, detailed sound whether you are playing a game or listening to music. The tuning is neither bass-heavy nor shrill, and the default equalizer sounds punchy and pleasant; owners can still adjust it through the PS5 interface. Sony has used this high-end driver design before, starting in 2023, but never at such a low price.

在它毫不起眼的耳罩里，装着定制的平面磁驱动单元，无论你是在打游戏还是听音乐，都能提供干净、细腻的声音。它的调音既不过分强调低频，也不尖利刺耳，默认的均衡器听起来饱满悦耳；用户仍可在 PS5 界面里自行调整。索尼早在 2023 年就用过这种高端驱动设计，但从没有做到这么低的价位。
01:02

03The fit

Comfort is the second strength. The headband, available in white or black, has a soft-touch underside and enough give to spread the weight across the top of your head. The headset itself is light, and its size adjustments extend far beyond what most people will ever need. The ear cups can even rotate 180 degrees in either direction, a feature that is unusual in this price bracket and genuinely useful when you rest the headset around your neck.

舒适是它的第二个强项。头梁有白色和黑色两种，内侧为柔软的触感材质，头顶承重处留有足够弹性，能把重量分散开。耳机本身很轻，尺寸调节范围也远超大多数人的需要。耳罩甚至可以向任意方向旋转 180 度——在这个价位区间并不常见，而当你把耳机挂在脖子上时，这个设计确实好用。
01:38

04The connections

Functionally, the Pulse streams audio over two connections at once: PlayStation Link, Sony's low-latency, lossless wireless standard, and ordinary Bluetooth. A small USB-C dongle pairs it quickly with a PS5, a PC or a Nintendo Switch 2. A dedicated Bluetooth button pauses or resumes Bluetooth content and can also answer calls, while a volume rocker controls Bluetooth audio whenever no Link device is connected. In simultaneous mode, the same rocker adjusts only the device attached to the dongle.

在功能上，Pulse 可以同时通过两条连接串流音频：一条是 PlayStation Link（索尼的低延迟无损无线标准），另一条是普通蓝牙。它附带一个小巧的 USB-C 适配器，插上 PS5、个人电脑或任天堂 Switch 2 就能快速配对。专用蓝牙按键除了配对，还能暂停或继续蓝牙音源（也可以接听、挂断电话）；而音量拨轮只在没有 Link 设备连接时才控制蓝牙音量。在同时播放模式下，同一拨轮只调节接在适配器上的设备。
02:15

05The lineup

Owners of the PlayStation Portal handheld can connect the Pulse directly, without the dongle at all. Nor is the Pulse Sony's only new model. The Pulse Edge, which still has no launch date or price, will pack twice as many magnets into its drivers, add active noise cancellation and a detachable boom mic, and include game and chat controls. It will clearly cost more.

拥有 PlayStation Portal 掌机的用户可以直接连接 Pulse，完全不需要适配器。Pulse 也不是索尼唯一的新型号。Pulse Edge 至今没有公布上市日期和价格，但它会把驱动单元里的磁体数量翻倍，加入主动降噪和可拆卸悬臂麦克风，并提供游戏与语音聊天音量控制。它显然会更贵。
02:45

06The verdict

Judged purely on features, the $99 Pulse is light on features compared with its rivals. Yet the company's decision to spend the budget on sound quality and comfort makes sense, because those are the qualities players notice every single day. For PS5 owners on a strict budget, it is an easy recommendation.

如果只看功能清单，99 美元的 Pulse 相比同价位对手确实功能偏少。但索尼把预算花在音质和舒适度上的决定是合理的，因为这两点正是玩家每天都能感受到的东西。对于预算紧张的 PS5 用户来说，它是个很容易做出的推荐。
