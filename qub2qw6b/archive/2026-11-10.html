Earth Is Trapping Heat at a Record Pace, UN Warns as Cop31 Opens

The Guardian · 约 3 分钟
导读　世界气象组织在土耳其安塔利亚召开的 Cop31 气候峰会开幕之际发布报告，指出地球气候系统正以有记录以来最快的速度积聚热量，2026 年预计将成为史上最热的四个年份之一。报告强调，地球能量收支失衡达到监测开始以来的最高水平，海洋、海冰和冰川都在刷新纪录。联合国秘书长古特雷斯警告，人类已进入“气候极端化的新时代”。

Heat is building up inside Earth's climate system faster than at any time since records began, the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) warned as the Cop31 summit opened on Monday in the Turkish resort city of Antalya. The UN agency's latest State of the Global Climate update says 2026 is on course to become one of the four warmest years ever measured, with the other three falling between 2023 and 2025.

世界气象组织（WMO）警告说，地球气候系统内部积聚热量的速度已达到有记录以来的最高水平。这一警告发出之际，Cop31 气候峰会于周一在土耳其度假城市安塔利亚开幕。这家联合国机构最新的《全球气候状况》更新报告指出，2026 年有望成为有观测记录以来最热的四个年份之一，而另外三个年份都落在 2023 至 2025 年之间。

The report focuses on a measure known as Earth's energy imbalance: the gap between the energy arriving from the sun and the energy the planet radiates back into space. That gap has reached its highest level since monitoring started. Because the atmosphere now holds more greenhouse gases, incoming heat stays roughly the same while outgoing heat steadily declines, so the world keeps a growing share of it. The WMO calculates that the imbalance increased by 0.3 watts per square metre per decade between 2001 and 2025, compared with 0.13 watts across the longer period from 1960.

报告聚焦于一项名为“地球能量失衡”的指标，即来自太阳的能量与地球向太空辐射回去的能量之间的差额。这一差额已达到监测开始以来的最高水平。由于大气中现在含有更多温室气体，入射的热量大致不变，而散出的热量却持续减少，于是地球留住了越来越大比例的热量。世界气象组织计算，2001 至 2025 年间，这一失衡每十年增加每平方米 0.3 瓦；相比之下，从 1960 年起的更长时段内，每十年仅增加 0.13 瓦。

The consequences are already visible. From January to September, average surface temperatures were about 1.47C above the pre-industrial baseline. Sea surface temperatures broke monthly records, sea level reached its highest point on record, and the ocean, which absorbs about 90% of the excess heat trapped by greenhouse gases, is heading for a tenth consecutive year of record warmth. Antarctic sea ice recorded its third-lowest annual maximum, and glaciers lost more than 400 gigatonnes of mass — the fourth year in a row in which all 19 glacierised regions shrank.

后果已经显现。1 月至 9 月，平均地表温度比工业化前基线高出约 1.47 摄氏度。海表温度打破月度纪录，海平面达到有记录以来的最高点；海洋吸收了温室气体所困住的约 90% 的多余热量，如今正迈向连续第十年创纪录的温暖。南极海冰的年最大范围创下历史第三低，冰川则损失了超过 4000 亿吨的质量——这是全球全部 19 个冰川区域连续第四年同时出现质量亏损。

UN secretary general Antonio Guterres said the findings showed that we have entered a new age of climate extremes. He warned that a supersized El Nino is colliding with a planet already heated by human activity. The year began with La Nina, which usually lowers the global average by 0.1-0.2C, yet a strong El Nino is now forming, so the final figure for 2026 is likely to exceed 1.47C.

联合国秘书长古特雷斯表示，这些发现表明人类已经进入“气候极端化的新时代”。他警告说，一个“超级厄尔尼诺”正与一颗已被人类活动加热的星球相撞。今年年初是拉尼娜状态，通常会使全球平均气温降低 0.1 至 0.2 摄氏度；但如今一个强厄尔尼诺正在形成，因此 2026 年的最终数值很可能超过 1.47 摄氏度。

Scientists argue that the imbalance, rather than annual emissions alone, is the clearest signal of how quickly warming will continue. Bill McGuire, emeritus professor of geophysical and climate hazards at University College London, said natural feedbacks are now pushing temperatures up faster than they did 20 years ago. He expects the 1.5C limit to be crossed permanently around 2028 and 2C in the late 2030s, adding that the widely quoted projection of 2.6-2.8C by the end of the century now looks wildly optimistic.

科学家认为，相比单纯的年度排放量，这一能量失衡才是衡量变暖将持续多快的最清晰信号。伦敦大学学院地球物理与气候灾害荣休教授比尔·麦圭尔说，自然反馈机制如今正以比 20 年前更快的速度推高气温。他预计 1.5 摄氏度的上限将在 2028 年前后被永久突破，2 摄氏度则会在 2030 年代后期被突破；他还表示，被广泛引用的“本世纪末升温 2.6 至 2.8 摄氏度”的预测如今看来“过于乐观”，甚至根本不可能。

Only 6.4% of Stroke Survivors Get the Therapy They Need

The Guardian · 约 3 分钟
导读　英国中风协会援引最新审计报告指出，在英格兰、威尔士和北爱尔兰，只有 6.4% 的中风幸存者获得了国民医疗服务体系本应提供的康复治疗时长，比例较 2024 年底进一步下降。报告称，康复不足严重影响了患者恢复身体与认知功能的机会，而中风每年给英国造成约 257 亿英镑的损失。

Fewer than one in 15 stroke survivors in England, Wales and Northern Ireland receive the amount of rehabilitation therapy the NHS is supposed to provide, according to a new report that the Stroke Association called a damning indictment of stroke care. The charity says the shortage of therapy seriously damages patients' chances of regaining physical and mental function.

一份最新报告显示，在英格兰、威尔士和北爱尔兰，不到十五分之一的中风幸存者获得了国民医疗服务体系（NHS）本应提供的康复治疗量。英国中风协会称，这份报告是对中风护理状况的“严厉控诉”。该慈善机构表示，治疗不足严重损害了患者恢复身体与认知功能的机会。

Guidelines from the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence state that every stroke patient should receive three hours a day of physiotherapy, speech and language therapy and occupational therapy, at least five days a week, whether they are still in hospital or recovering at home. In practice, the Sentinel Stroke National Audit Programme (SSNAP) found that only 6.4% of patients met that standard between January and March this year, down from 7.5% in late 2024.

英国国家卫生与临床优化研究所的指南规定，每位中风患者都应每天接受三小时的物理治疗、言语治疗和职业治疗，每周至少五天，无论他们仍在住院还是已经回家继续康复。但在实际操作中，“哨兵中风国家审计项目”（SSNAP）发现，今年 1 月至 3 月间只有 6.4% 的患者达到了这一标准，低于 2024 年底的 7.5%。

The average amount of therapy was 40 minutes a day, six minutes less than in the previous quarter. Some patients received just 15.2 minutes, and even the best-served received only 57.3 minutes. Only 68% continued to receive rehabilitation support after leaving hospital. Because stroke is the leading cause of complex disability in the UK, such gaps can leave survivors struggling to walk, speak or swallow for the rest of their lives. About 100,000 people a year have a stroke, and an ageing population with more high blood pressure and diabetes could push the figure to 115,483 by 2030.

患者平均每天只获得 40 分钟治疗，比上一季度少了 6 分钟。有些患者仅得到 15.2 分钟，即便是获得服务最多的患者也只有 57.3 分钟。仅有 68% 的人在出院后继续获得康复支持。由于中风是英国复杂残疾的首要成因，这样的缺口可能让幸存者在余生中行走、说话或吞咽都十分困难。英国每年约有 10 万人中风，而人口老龄化以及高血压、糖尿病患者增多，可能使这一数字到 2030 年升至 115483 人。

Juliet Bouverie, chief executive of the Stroke Association, said the figures exposed the deeply troubling reality that too many vulnerable stroke survivors are being left without the care and support they need. She argued that the health system treats stroke as an emergency but neglects the long recovery that follows, even though the brain injury can change every aspect of a person's life in an instant.

中风协会首席执行官朱丽叶·布弗里表示，这些数字揭示了一个令人深感不安的现实：太多脆弱的中风幸存者得不到他们所需的照护与支持。她认为，医疗体系把中风当作急症来处理，却忽视了随之而来的漫长康复过程，尽管脑损伤可能在一瞬间改变一个人生活的方方面面。

The charity estimates that stroke costs the UK about 25.7bn pounds a year. Social care accounts for 13.7bn pounds, while wider costs such as lost work and relatives giving up jobs add another 6.7bn pounds. The Department of Health and Social Care did not address the rehabilitation shortfall directly. Instead it stressed NHS efforts to speed up treatment, including a pledge to cut premature deaths from heart disease and stroke by a quarter within a decade, and pointed to rising rates of thrombolysis and thrombectomy, procedures that dissolve or remove blood clots.

该慈善机构估计，中风每年给英国造成约 257 亿英镑的损失。其中社会照护支出占 137 亿英镑；而更广泛的社会成本，例如患者无法工作、亲属不得不放弃工作来照顾他们，又增加了 67 亿英镑。英国卫生与社会保障部没有直接回应康复治疗不足的问题，而是强调 NHS 为加快治疗所做的努力，包括承诺在十年内将心脏病和中风导致的过早死亡减少四分之一，并指出溶栓和血栓切除术的比例正在上升——这两类手术分别用于溶解或取出血栓。
