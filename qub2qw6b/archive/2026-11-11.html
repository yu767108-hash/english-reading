US Inflation Comes In Higher Than Expected in October

BBC News · 约 3 分钟
导读　美国 10 月消费者价格指数环比上涨 0.4%，高于分析师预期的 0.3%，中期选举刚过一周便给美联储 12 月加息增添了理由。文章解释了通胀如何用「一篮子商品」衡量、需求与供给如何推高物价，以及关税为何让美国企业承担了近九成成本。

Prices in the United States rose faster than expected last month, official figures show. The consumer price index climbed 0.4% in October, down from 0.6% in September but ahead of the 0.3% that analysts had predicted. The surprise reading lands a week after midterm elections dominated by the cost of living, and it may strengthen the case for the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates when it meets in December.析

美国官方数据显示，上月物价上涨速度快于预期。10 月消费者价格指数上涨 0.4%，低于 9 月的 0.6%，但高于分析师预测的 0.3%。这个出人意料的读数出现在中期选举一周之后——那场选举的焦点正是生活成本——它可能增强美联储在 12 月会议上加息的论据。

Inflation measures how quickly prices rise over time. If a bottle of milk costs $1 today and $1.10 a year from now, annual inflation for that bottle is 10%.析 The Bureau of Labor Statistics tracks this by following a basket of everyday items - fresh food, petrol, housing and medical care - and updating the basket as spending habits change.

通货膨胀衡量的是物价随时间的上涨速度。如果一瓶牛奶今天卖 1 美元，一年后涨到 1.10 美元，那么这瓶牛奶的年通胀率就是 10%。美国劳工统计局通过跟踪一篮子日常商品——新鲜食品、汽油、住房和医疗服务——并随着消费习惯的变化不断更新这个篮子，来衡量通胀。

Several forces push prices up. When demand outstrips supply, producers can charge more; the craze for Labubu dolls and their sky-high resale prices is one example. Higher incomes can fuel demand in the same way. Supply shocks matter too: crude oil traded just above $70 a barrel before the US-Israel war with Iran, and as the conflict constricted supplies the price jumped past $100.

推高物价的因素有好几个。当需求超过供给时，生产商就可以提高价格；近年来拉布布娃娃的热潮及其高得离谱的转售价格就是一个例子。收入提高同样会刺激需求，进而推高价格。供给冲击也很重要：在与伊朗开战之前，原油价格略高于每桶 70 美元，而随着冲突收紧了供应，油价迅速突破 100 美元。

October's increase was driven by housing, food and energy. Energy costs, however, rose just 0.7%, a far cry from September's 5.2% jump. Because the bureau did not collect data in October 2025, it could not publish a 12-month figure for last month; the rate for the year to September stood at 3.7%.

10 月的涨幅主要由住房、食品和能源成本推动。不过能源价格仅上涨 0.7%，远低于 9 月 5.2% 的涨幅。由于劳工统计局没有收集 2025 年 10 月的数据，因此无法给出上月的 12 个月同比数据；截至 9 月的年度通胀率为 3.7%。

Tariffs are part of the story. President Trump has placed levies on most trading partners, arguing that they would raise tax revenue and revive manufacturing. Research by the New York Fed suggests exporters barely cut their prices in response, so American firms absorbed almost 90% of the added cost.析 Every one percentage point rise in average tariffs, the study found, pushed consumer prices up by nearly 0.25% within a year.

关税也是其中一环。特朗普总统对大多数贸易伙伴加征了关税，称这将增加税收、重振制造业。纽约联储的研究显示，出口商几乎没有因此降价，因此美国企业承担了近 90% 的额外成本。研究发现，平均关税每提高 1 个百分点，一年内就会把消费品价格推高近 0.25%。

Inflation is still above the Fed's 2% target, though far below the roughly 9% peak reached in 2022, when pandemic supply chains buckled and oil surged after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Slower inflation does not mean falling prices; it means prices are climbing more gently.析 Central banks generally regard about 2% as healthy - high enough to keep shoppers spending, low enough to stay out of their decisions.

通胀仍高于美联储 2% 的目标，但远低于 2022 年约 9% 的峰值——当时疫情扰乱了供应链，俄罗斯全面入侵乌克兰又令油价飙升。通胀放缓并不意味着物价下跌，而是意味着物价上涨得更温和。各国央行通常认为 2% 左右是健康的：足以让人们继续消费，又低到不会左右他们的决定。
